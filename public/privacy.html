Kojlux Study Hub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kojlux Study Hub collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Kojlux Study Hub website or app.

Kojlux Study Hub is a study workspace for creating learning materials, reviewing flashcards, organizing collections, and sharing selected study materials with other people.

Important: Please do not upload private, confidential, or sensitive information unless you have permission to use it for study. A shared flashcard or collection can be viewed by anyone who receives its sharing link.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address and account identity supplied through Firebase Authentication.
  • Study information: quiz answers and results, study history, flashcards, review schedules, saved collections, exam reminders, grade level, and study activity.
  • Content you provide: prompts, notes, images, PDFs, videos, educational links, and other materials you choose to upload or submit.
  • Shared content: flashcards or collections you intentionally publish through a share link.
  • Device and app information: limited technical information needed for authentication, local storage, notifications, and app functionality.

How We Use Information

  • Provide account access and keep your study data available across signed-in devices.
  • Generate quizzes, summaries, study explanations, visualizations, and review suggestions from the prompts or materials you submit.
  • Save your flashcards, collections, exam reminders, preferences, and review progress.
  • Display community or shared study materials when you choose to publish them.
  • Send in-app or browser notifications when you enable them, such as review and exam reminders.
  • Protect, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

Service Providers

Kojlux Study Hub uses third-party services to operate the app. These may process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase by Google: authentication, database storage, file storage, and related app infrastructure.
  • Google Gemini: processing prompts or study materials when you request generated quizzes, summaries, explanations, or visualizations.
  • Other content services: certain image or educational-link features may request content from external services when you use those features.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with the service providers described above and when disclosure is needed to comply with law, protect the service, or prevent misuse.

Content remains private within your account unless you use a sharing or community feature. Anyone with a shared-card or shared-collection link may be able to view the content included in that link.

Storage and Retention

Account data and saved study content are retained while needed to provide the service and while your account remains active. Some preferences, notification history, and draft data may also be stored locally in your browser or device. You can remove local data by clearing the app's site storage.

When you request account or content deletion, we will take reasonable steps to remove the applicable data, subject to information we must keep for security, legal, or operational reasons and to any data retained by third-party providers under their own policies.

Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to create an account or use available guest features.
  • You can choose whether to enable browser notifications.
  • You can edit or remove saved flashcards, collections, reminders, and shared content where those controls are available.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of account information by contacting the Kojlux Study Hub team at officialkojux@gmail.com.

Children

Kojlux Study Hub is intended for students and educators and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact the Kojlux Study Hub team so it can be reviewed and removed.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use care when uploading or sharing study materials.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made. Continued use of the service after an update means the revised policy applies.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Kojlux Study Hub team at officialkojux@gmail.com.

This page describes the current app behavior and should be reviewed with the publisher's legal and store-compliance requirements before release.
